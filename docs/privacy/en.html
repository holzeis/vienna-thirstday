Privacy Policy

Effective 8 September 2026

Vienna Thirstday is a small, non-commercial pickup-football scheduling app for a private group of amateur players who meet on Thursdays. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and what rights you have over it under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — regardless of the app being run for free, those rights still apply.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Richard Holzeis, who builds and runs Vienna Thirstday for the group.
Contact for anything on this page: vienna-thirstday@holzeis.me

2. What information we collect

  • Name — required to use the app and to appear on rosters and results.
  • Password — stored as a secure hash, never in plain text.
  • Email address — optional. Used only by the organizers to contact you about games. Never shown to other players.
  • Profile photo — optional.
  • Match history — which matchdays you registered for, which team you played on, and the points/goal difference you earned.
  • Usage information — each time you log in, sign up as a guest via a share link, or open the installed app, we record that event together with your device's operating system, browser, and device type (read from your browser's standard "User-Agent" header). This is used only to understand how the app is used — queried directly by the organizer, never shown inside the app itself.
  • Push notification details — only if you explicitly enable notifications in your browser, we store the technical subscription details needed to deliver them.

If a fellow player brings you along as a guest without you creating your own account, we still hold your name and match record. You have the same rights over that data as anyone else — see section 6.

3. Why we process this information, and on what legal basis

  • Organizing matchdays, teams, and season standings — our legitimate interest in running the league (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Contacting you about games via the email you provided — your consent, given voluntarily when you added it (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
  • Understanding how the app is used (the usage information above) — our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Sending push notifications — your consent, given through your browser's notification permission prompt (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

4. Who else sees your data

  • Cloudflare — routes traffic to the app's server (a tunnel/proxy service) and sees connection metadata.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Mozilla, or Apple) — only if you enable notifications, to relay them to your device.

Nobody else. The app is hosted on the organizer's own server; no data is sold, rented, or used for advertising. Some of the providers above are based outside the EU/EEA (e.g. in the United States); where that's the case, they participate in recognized data-transfer safeguards such as the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

5. How long we keep your data

Match history and season records are kept indefinitely, as the shared record of the league. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your password and any email/photo on file — your name and match history remain (in the same state as any other guest player) unless you specifically ask us to also erase your name from that history, which we can do on request (see section 6).

6. Your rights

Under GDPR, you can ask us (vienna-thirstday@holzeis.me) at any time to:

  • see what data we hold about you (access),
  • correct anything that's wrong (rectification),
  • delete your data — including anonymizing your name in historical match records, if you want full erasure rather than just closing your account (erasure),
  • object to a particular use of your data, or ask us to restrict it (objection / restriction),
  • receive a copy of your data in a portable format (portability).

We'll respond within 30 days. You can also lodge a complaint at any time with Austria's data protection authority, the Datenschutzbehörde.

7. Cookies

This app does not use cookies. It stores your login session in your browser's local storage, which is required for the app to work and is never used for tracking or advertising.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally; the effective date above will change accordingly.
